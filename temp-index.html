Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

How does Briefing work?

Briefing uses advanced AI to analyze your raw notes and synthesize them into structured briefings. It identifies key themes, extracts action items, and organizes information into clear sections. Just paste your notes and get results in seconds.

What file formats are supported?

Briefing works with any text format - plain text, markdown, Word documents, PDFs, and more. You can paste text directly, upload files, or connect to your favorite note-taking apps. Audio transcripts are also supported.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. Your notes are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never use your data to train our AI models. Your data remains yours - we process it and delete it after a set period. You can request immediate deletion at any time.

How much does it cost?

Briefing uses a credit-based pricing model. You pay only for what you use - no monthly subscriptions. During early access, we're offering generous credit packages at a discount. Full pricing will be announced before launch.

Can I customize the briefing format?

Yes! Briefing offers multiple templates for different use cases (product, engineering, client meetings, etc.). You can also create custom templates to match your team's workflow exactly.

Is there a team plan?

Team features are coming soon. During early access, you can share briefings with your team. We're building collaborative features like shared workspaces, team templates, and role-based access for launch.

When will Briefing launch?

We're currently in early access and working closely with beta users to improve the product. Public launch is planned for Q2 2026. Early access users get lifetime discounts and priority access to new features.
